Pin both extensions in the toolbar

Chrome does not allow an extension to pin itself (or another extension) to the toolbar. You have to pin once by hand.

Bubble them out of the puzzle menu so SetupVPN + this reconnector stay visible:

  1. Click the puzzle piece in the Chrome toolbar
  2. Find SetupVPN - Lifetime Free VPN → click the pin
  3. Find SetupVPN Sweden Reconnector → click the pin

Why

SetupVPN’s active link often comes from its toolbar icon. The reconnector shows status/time remaining and opens the dashboard sidebar from its icon.

After pinning, keep a SetupVPN dashboard tab (or the sidebar) open while connected.
